# Sound credits

`page-turn-1.m4a` … `page-turn-5.m4a` are trimmed, level-matched AAC encodes of
recordings from **"Book Flip Sounds" by Voltiment555**, published on OpenGameArt
under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication; no attribution required).

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/book-flip-sounds
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

| --- | --- |

| page-turn-2.m4a | BookFlip6.wav |
| page-turn-3.m4a | BookFlip10.wav |
| page-turn-4.m4a | BookFlip11.wav |
| page-turn-5.m4a | BookFlip2.wav |

Processing: mono, 70 Hz high-pass, trimmed to one turn, RMS level-matched,
short fades, AAC 112 kbps.
