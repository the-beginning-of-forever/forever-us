My Love, My Forever ❤️

Na Muddula Pallam, ❤️

Mana life lo oka beautiful journey start cheyyabothunnam. Ee journey start avvakamundhu, na heart lo unna konni feelings neetho share chesukovali anukuntunnanu.

Mana life lo prathi roju happy ga untundhani, manaki eppudu problems raakudadani nenu promise cheyyalenu. Life lo misunderstandings, financial problems, responsibilities, unexpected situations anni ravachu.

Kaani okati maatram manaspoorthiga promise chesthunnanu.

Life lo em situation vachina, nuvvu eppudu ontariga face cheyyalsina avasaram lekunda nenu nee pakkana untaanu.

Nuvvu happy ga unna rojullo nee smile ki reason avvalani anukuntunnanu. Nuvvu badha padina rojullo nee tears ki shoulder avvalani anukuntunnanu. Nuvvu tired ga unna appudu nee comfort zone ga undalani anukuntunnanu.

World motham neeku against ga unna kuda, nee pakkana okaru unnaru ani neeku eppudu anipinchali. Aa okarini nene avvalani korukuntunnanu.

Nee Father's Love ❤️

Nee life lo mee father love ni evaru replace cheyyaleru. Nenu kuda aa place ni replace cheyyalenu. Father iche care, protection, affection chaala special. Aa love ki evaru comparison kaaleru.

Kaani nenu okati promise chesthunnanu.

Aayana neeku ichina care, protection, security, happiness ni naa way lo neeku ivvadaniki naa lifetime try chesthanu.

Nenu aayana place ni teesukolenu, kaani neeku oka feeling maatram ivvalani anukuntunnanu — "Nenu naa husband daggara kuda safe ga unnaanu, naa feelings ni ardham chesukune oka person naa life lo unnadu" ani.

Nuvvu eppudu special ga, valued ga, loved ga feel avvalani naa korika.

Nee Family – Naa Family ❤️

Inko important promise neeku cheyyali anukuntunnanu.

Nenu ninnu marriage chesukuntunnanu ante, ninnu maatrame naa life loki teesukuntunnanu ani kaadhu. Ninnu penchina nee family ni kuda naa family ga accept chesthunnanu.

Nee parents happiness, health, responsibilities, problems anni naaku kuda important.

Nenu vaalla family lo puttakapovachu, kaani vaalla heart lo oka kodukuga place sampadinchalani anukuntunnanu.

Nenu just son-in-law ga maatrame undalani anukovatledhu. Vaallaki oka kodukuga, vaalla happiness lo oka part ga, vaallaki avasaram vachinappudu support ga undalani anukuntunnanu.

Nuvvu nee family ni vadilesi naa daggariki vachav ani eppudu feel avvakudadhu. Endhukante marriage ante nee family ni vadileyadam kaadhu. Inko family ni nee life loki add chesukovadam.

Nee family responsibilities ni nenu burden ga chudanu. Avi naa responsibilities kuda. Vaallani naa family laage care chesukuntaanu.

Nuvvu happy ga undali, nee parents happy ga undali. Naa valla nee family lo kuda oka happiness, oka support add avvali. Adhe naa korika.

Mana Beautiful Memories ❤️

Naaku mana life just office ki velladam, intiki raavadam, dinner cheyyadam, padukovadam ane routine la undakudadhu.

Manam life ni enjoy cheyyali. Chinna chinna moments ni beautiful memories ga marchukovali.

Nuvvu ready ga unte, destination lekunda long drives ki vellali. Late-night rides ki vellali. Manaki ishtamaina songs vintuu, random topics gurinchi maatladukuntu ala travel cheyyali.

Appudappudu small trips plan cheyyali. New places explore cheyyali. Different foods try cheyyali. Chala photos teesukovali. Konni silly memories create chesukovali.

Ninnu appudappudu surprise cheyyali. Flowers ivvali, small gifts ivvali, candlelight dinners plan cheyyali. Special occasions kosame kaadhu, normal days ni kuda special ga celebrate cheyyali.

Nee office lo entha stress unna, intiki vachaka konchem relax avvali.

Office lo jarigina things anni naatho share chesukovali. Evaraina irritate chesina, work pressure unna, bad day aina, anni naatho cheppukovali.

Manam late-night conversations chesukovali. Konni hours maatladukovali. Movies chudali. Kalisi navvukovali.

Konni saarlu em maatladakunda pakkana pakkana kurchuni peaceful ga time spend cheyyali.

Nee worst day kuda naa daggara ki vachaka konchem better ga anipinchali. Naa company lo nee stress marchipoyi peaceful ga feel avvali.

Naaku prathi moment extraordinary ga undalsina avasaram ledhu. Nuvvu naatho unte ordinary moments kuda special avuthayi.

Mana Future, Mana Dreams ❤️

Appudappudu mana future gurinchi imagine chesukuntuntaanu.

Manam kalisi breakfast cheyyadam, birthdays celebrate chesukovadam, mana home ni decorate chesukovadam, random outings ki velladam, family photos teesukovadam... ila enno chinna chinna dreams unnayi.

Future lo manaki children puttaka, vallaki love, respect, kindness, understanding unna beautiful family environment ivvalani anukuntunnanu.

Nenu oka good husband ga, responsible father ga undalani anukuntunnanu.

Mana children mana iddarini chusi love ante enti, respect ante enti, family ante enti nerchukovali.

Manam okarinokaru ela care chesukuntunnamo, ela support chesukuntunnamo vallu chudali.

Naaku luxurious life kanna happy family important.

Manam andaram kalisi navvukuntu, happy ga, peaceful ga unde oka beautiful home create chesukovali.

Trust, Understanding & Respect ❤️

Mana madhya edaina problem vachinappudu silent ga undakunda maatladukovali.

Small misunderstandings ni big distances ga marchukokudadhu.

Manam arguments chesukunna, okari meeda okariki kopam vachina, mana madhya unna love ni marchipokudadhu.

Financial problems vachina kalisi face cheddam.

Life difficult ga unna kalisi solution find cheddam.

Okaru weak ga feel ayithe inkokaru strength ga undali.

Okaru mistake chesthe, hurt chesukuntu undakunda ardham chesukoni correct chesukundham.

Mana ego kanna mana relationship important.

Nenu perfect person ni kaadhu. Naa valla kuda mistakes avvachu. Konni saarlu ninnu correct ga ardham chesukolekapovachu.

Kaani naa mistakes accept chesukoni, ninnu ardham chesukuntu, better husband ga maaradaniki prathi roju try chesthanu.

Nuvvu naa daggara happy ga unnappude kaadhu, sad ga unna, angry ga unna, upset ga unna kuda comfortable ga undali.

Nuvvu nee feelings ni hide chesukovalsina avasaram ledhu.

Naa daggara nuvvu ela unna, ninnu alage accept chesukune person ni nenu avvalani anukuntunnanu.

Forever – I Choose You ❤️

Nenu prathi roju happy ga unchuthanu ani promise cheyyalenu, kaani prathi situation lo nee pakkana untaanu ani promise chesthunnanu.

Mana life lo problems raakunda chusthanu ani promise cheyyalenu, kaani vachina prathi problem ni kalisi face cheddam ani promise chesthunnanu.

Nenu eppudu mistakes cheyyanu ani cheppalenu, kaani naa mistakes nunchi nerchukoni better husband ga maaruthanu ani promise chesthunnanu.

Life eppudu easy ga untundhani cheppalenu, kaani life difficult ga unna nuvvu okkadanive face cheyyalsina situation raakunda chusthanu.

Ninnu expensive gifts tho, big surprises tho maatrame happy ga unchali ani anukovatledhu.

Nenu ninnu vinadam, nee feelings ni respect cheyyadam, chinna chinna things remember chesukovadam, nee happiness kosam efforts pettadam, prathi roju ninnu choose chesukovadam... ivanni naa love lo part ga undali.

Oka roju manam old ayyaka, mana past ni gurthuchesukuntu ila anukovali:

"Mana life perfect ga lekapovachu, kaani manam okariki okaram unnam. Enno trips vellam, enno memories create chesukunnam, kalisi navvukunnam, kalisi edcham, enno problems face chesam. Kaani eppudu okarinokaram vadileyaledhu."

Naaku nuvvu just naa life lo oka person kaadhu.

Nuvvu naa future. Nuvvu naa happiness. Nuvvu naatho kalisi mana family ni build cheyyaboye person.

Nee tho kalisi dreams fulfil chesukovali. Nee tho kalisi responsibilities share chesukovali. Nee family ni naa family la care chesukovali. Nee tho kalisi life lo enno beautiful memories create chesukovali.

Chivariga okati cheppali...

Nuvvu naa life loki vachav kabatti naa life beautiful avuthundhi ani maatrame kaadhu. Nee life loki vachina nenu kuda nee life ni beautiful ga marchadaniki naa best isthanu.

Eeroju, repu, happy situations lo, difficult situations lo, mana life lo prathi stage lo...

I CHOOSE YOU. AND I WILL KEEP CHOOSING YOU, EVERY SINGLE DAY. ❤️

Kaani aa moment lo naa feelings anni neetho cheppalekapovachu.

Andhuke ippude neeku okati cheppali anukuntunnanu.

Please remember one thing, Bangaram... ❤️

♥Nuvvu nee parents ni vadilesi naa life loki vasthunnav ani eppudu anukoku. Nee parents eppatiki nee parents ye. Nee family eppatiki nee family ye. Vallatho maatladadaniki, vallani kalavadaniki, vallani care cheyyadaniki nuvvu eppudu permission adagalsina avasaram ledhu.

Kaani ippudu neeku inko family kuda untundhi — adhi naa family. ❤️

♥Naa parents nee parents laage. Naa family lo prathi okkaru nee family members avutharu. Nuvvu happy ga unna, sad ga unna, edaina problem vachina, nee husband maatrame kaadhu, naa family kuda nee pakkana untundhi ani nuvvu feel avvali.

♥Nuvvu maa intiki vachina guest vi kaadhu. Nuvvu maa family lo oka part. Nee happiness, nee dreams, nee feelings, nee responsibilities anni maaku important.

♥Nuvvu nee parents daggara ela free ga, comfortable ga untavo, alage naa family daggara kuda comfortable ga undali. Nee opinions cheppadaniki, nee feelings share chesukovadaniki eppudu hesitate avvakudadhu.
